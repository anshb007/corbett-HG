Dhikala Forest Lodge

Dhikala is the most popular rest house in Jim Corbett National Park. Situated in the heart of the core wildlife zone, Dhikala is surrounded by the Ramganga river channel, massive riverine forests, and the vast Dhikala Chaur (grassland). Staying here offers the rawest and most authentic jungle experience available in India.

Built during the British raj, the complex features historic brick cabins, wooden cabins, and dormitory beds. Because it sits inside the park perimeter, guests can enjoy exclusive morning and evening jeep safaris and tiger spotting treks directly starting from the campus.
