Marry in the Wilderness

Unforgettable Forest Ceremonies

Imagine exchanging your vows to the sound of flowing rivers, chirping forest birds, and rustling sal leaves. Jim Corbett offers some of the most romantic riverside and wilderness luxury resorts for destination weddings.
