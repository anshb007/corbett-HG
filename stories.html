Tête-à-Tête with the Royal Bengal Tiger in Dhikala

"It was our third safari drive inside Dhikala zone when our guide suddenly pointed at the dry riverbed. Out stepped a massive male Bengal Tiger. He walked calmly along our vehicle path for a solid ten minutes, giving us once-in-a-lifetime camera shots. The scale of the grasslands and raw wilderness here is truly unmatched."

Birding Trail
Nov 20, 2025 Dr. Sarah Miller

A Paradise for Birdwatchers: My Jhirna Diary

"Corbett is just as famous for birds as it is for tigers. In Jhirna zone, we spotted Crested Serpent Eagles, Great Pied Hornbills, Plumbeous Water Redstarts, and various kingfishers. The guide's knowledge of calls and birding hotspots was phenomenal. A must-visit for any serious birder!"
