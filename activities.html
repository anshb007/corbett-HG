Corbett Safari Zones

Explore the Core Tourism Zones

The national park is divided into distinct eco-tourism zones. Each offers a unique landscape, vegetation type, and wildlife sighting probability.

Most Popular

Dhikala Tourism Zone

Famed for its vast grasslands (Chaur) and scenic Ramganga river channel. Dhikala offers the highest probability of tiger sightings and is the only zone offering Canter Safaris.

  • Tiger, Asiatic Elephant, Hog Deer
  • Canter Safari (Jeep allowed for lodge guests)
  • Open: 15th Nov to 15th June
Book Dhikala Safari
Scenic Trails

Bijrani Tourism Zone

Known for its scenic beauty, wide forest streams, and dense mixed vegetation. Bijrani was once a private shooting reserve for British dignitaries and remains a prime tiger habitat.

  • Tiger, Sloth Bear, Jackal, Spotted Deer
  • Jeep Safari Only
  • Open: 15th Oct to 30th June
Book Bijrani Safari
Year Round

Jhirna Tourism Zone

An eco-tourism zone added in 1994, Jhirna is situated on the southern boundary of the park. It features dry deciduous forests and holds a steady population of sloth bears and wild elephants.

  • Sloth Bear, Wild Elephant, Sambhar, Leopard
  • Jeep Safari Only
  • Open: All Year Round
Book Jhirna Safari
Year Round

Dhela Tourism Zone

Opened in 2014, Dhela is a beautiful buffer zone featuring diverse flora. It is highly popular for tiger sightings, multi-species deer herds, and bird watching.

  • Tiger, King Cobra, Barking Deer, Hornbills
  • Jeep Safari Only
  • Open: All Year Round

More Wilderness Activities

River Rafting

Ride the Class II rapids of the scenic Kosi River. Guided tours available during monsoons and high water flow seasons.

Guided Nature Treks

Walk along the buffer trails under the guidance of experienced local naturalists. Track pugmarks and learn about medicinal plants.

Bird Watching

Jim Corbett is home to over 600 species of migratory and local birds. Spot Great Pied Hornbills, Kingfishers, and fish eagles.

Corbett Waterfall

Visit the scenic 60-foot cascade tucked inside dense teak forests. Enjoy a quiet picnic spot and nature photography.
